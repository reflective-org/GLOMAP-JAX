1  The f32 reference is a different trajectory, not a noisier one

Column-scaled gap between the single- and double-precision reference runs, over 24 simulated hours. Three cases sit near 1e-3. marine_bcoc reaches 0.80 — the same model, the same namelist, a different answer. This is why ref-f64 is the only validation target (ADR-001).

2  Why marine_bcoc diverges: the Aitken insoluble mode

Ageing depletes that mode over seven orders of magnitude. In float64 number and mass leave in proportion and the mean dry diameter holds near 30 nm. In float32 the residual loses significance: the diameter collapses to 5.8 nm and the number stops decaying and turns back up. Two panels, not two y-axes on one.

The branch dump rules out a flipped predicate as the cause: of 108,432 branch records only 60 differ between the two variants, all from step 45 onward, while the trajectory has already diverged by step 20. It is cancellation, not a different code path.

3  What no shipped namelist ever reaches

Every predicate the gate-0 dump records, and how often it was true across all four cases. The bars at zero are the ones with no reference data at all — a port could get them arbitrarily wrong and no trajectory gate would notice. Reaching them is what the constructed phase-D fixtures are for.

4  Where the obvious spelling gives a different double

Fraction of 200,000 samples on which the natural JAX spelling disagrees with what gfortran computes. These are not rounding curiosities — each one cost this port a byte-equality failure, and two of them were found only after a test had already passed.

Read the last group carefully. x / 2.0 is at zero because the reciprocal of a power of two is exact, and true_divide is at zero because it is the fix. Everything between them is a live hazard.
